Local copies of the fonts used by the owner's panel design:

- DM Sans: Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans
- Plus Jakarta Sans: Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans
- Outfit: Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit
- Noto Serif SC: Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Serif+SC

The Latin WOFF2 subsets are distributed with their original SIL Open Font License
texts in `dmsans-OFL.txt`, `plusjakartasans-OFL.txt`, `outfit-OFL.txt` and
`notoserifsc-OFL.txt`. Noto Serif SC is the Google Fonts subset for the three
decorative characters used by the Panda Oriental design. Downloaded font binaries
are unmodified; the other files are Latin subsets.
The panel serves these files locally and does not request fonts from external servers.
